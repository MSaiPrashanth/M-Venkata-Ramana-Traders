About Venkat Ramana Traders

Who we are

A local kirana store for Kandi and around

Venkat Ramana Traders is a local grocery store serving customers in Kandi and the surrounding areas. Located right opposite the Post Office, we're a familiar stop for households looking for everyday groceries and home essentials.

Our goal is simple: keep the shelves stocked with the things people actually need — rice and grains, pulses and dals, cooking oils, spices, snacks, dairy, personal care and household cleaning items — and make sure customers are looked after when they walk in.

We're proud to be rated 4.5 out of 5 by customers on Google, and we look forward to welcoming you at the store.
